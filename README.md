# ReguLens: The Proactive Compliance Engine

## Executive Summary

ReguLens is an AI-native platform designed to transform the Tax, Accounting, and Audit Professional(TAAP) domain from a reactive research-based workflow to a proactive, agentic monitoring ecosystem. By leveraging intelligent automation, ReguLens ensures that no regulatory change in California, Texas, or Florida goes unanalyzed.

---

## The Vision

**To eliminate the "Manual Discovery Gap"** — the weeks-long delay between the publication of a new law and the identification of affected clients.

ReguLens acts as a continuous "Site Reliability Monitor" for the regulatory landscape, moving firms from **"Searching for Answers"** to **"Receiving Solutions."**

---

## Key Value Drivers

### 100% Coverage
Automated monitoring of fragmented government feeds (FTB, Comptroller, SOS) that humans cannot feasibly track daily.

### Agentic Triage
Moving beyond "Search" to "Action." The system identifies client impact autonomously using domain-specific AI that understands regulatory intent, not just keywords.

### Risk Mitigation
Real-time alerts for compliance breaches or missed tax-saving opportunities, preventing human errors like overlooking client eligibility for state R&D credits.

### Enterprise Ready
Built on a modular, scalable architecture that grows with jurisdiction expansion and firm needs.
