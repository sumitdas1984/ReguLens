## Overview

This document outlines the go-to-market (GTM) strategy for ReguLens, including target customers, positioning, channels, and launch plan.

**GTM Goal:** Acquire 10 paying customers within 3 months of MVP launch.

---

## Target Customer Segments

### Primary Segment (MVP Focus)

**Mid-Sized Accounting & Tax Firms**

**Profile:**
- Firm size: 50-500 employees
- SALT (State & Local Tax) practice
- Multi-state client base (especially CA, TX, FL)
- Currently using manual monitoring or legacy research tools
- Pain: Can't afford Bloomberg enterprise pricing, too large for pure manual

**Firmographic:**
- Geography: US (focus on CA, TX, FL markets initially)
- Revenue: $10M-$100M annually
- Decision maker: Tax Practice Leader, Managing Partner, COO

**Example Firms:**
- Regional CPA firms with multi-state practices
- Boutique tax advisory firms
- Mid-market accounting firms (non-Big 4)

---

### Secondary Segments (Post-MVP)

**Corporate Tax Departments**
- Fortune 1000 in-house tax teams
- Multi-state operations
- Compliance-heavy roles

**Large Accounting Firms (Enterprise)**
- Top 50 firms
- May need enterprise features (white-label, API)
- Higher price point, longer sales cycle

## Pricing Strategy

**[To be finalized after competitive research and user validation]**

### Pricing Model Options

**Option 1: Per-User Pricing**
- $200-$400/user/month
- Seat-based (common in SaaS)
- Easy to understand

**Option 2: Per-Firm Pricing**
- $2,000-$5,000/firm/month (unlimited users)
- Better for team-based workflows
- Higher ACV (Annual Contract Value)

**Option 3: Per-Client-Profile Pricing**
- $10-$20/client profile/month
- Aligns cost with value (more clients = more value)
- Variable pricing

**Recommendation:** [Pending research] - likely **Per-Firm** for simplicity and higher ACV

## Customer Acquisition Channels

### Phase 1: Direct Outreach (MVP Launch)

**Channel:** Direct email + LinkedIn outreach to target firms

**Approach:**
1. Build list of 100 target firms (CA/TX/FL regional firms)
2. Identify decision makers (Tax Practice Leaders)
3. Personalized outreach: "We built this for firms like yours..."
4. Offer free pilot program (30 days)

**Goal:** 10 pilot participants → 5 paying customers

---

### Phase 2: Content Marketing (Months 3-6)

**Channel:** SEO + thought leadership

**Tactics:**
- Blog posts: "How to Monitor CA Tax Changes Without Losing Your Mind"
- Case studies: "How [Firm X] Saved 15 Hours/Week"
- LinkedIn posts from founders
- Webinars: "The Future of Tax Compliance"

**Goal:** Inbound leads, establish thought leadership

---

### Phase 3: Partnerships (Months 6-12)

**Channel:** Partner with complementary solutions

**Potential Partners:**
- Tax software vendors (UltraTax, CCH) - integration partnerships
- State CPA societies - sponsorships, webinars
- Industry conferences - booth, speaking slots

**Goal:** Access to qualified audiences

---

### Phase 4: Paid Acquisition (Post Product-Market Fit)

**Channel:** Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads

**Approach:**
- Target keywords: "state tax monitoring", "regulatory compliance software"
- LinkedIn targeting: Tax Directors, Compliance Managers
- Retargeting website visitors

**Goal:** Scalable lead generation

## Sales Process

### For MVP (Low-Touch, Product-Led)

**Funnel:**
1. **Awareness:** Outreach / content / referral
2. **Interest:** Visit website, sign up for trial
3. **Evaluation:** 14-day free trial (self-serve onboarding)
4. **Purchase:** Upgrade to paid plan via Stripe
5. **Onboarding:** Email drip + help docs
6. **Expansion:** Upsell additional users or states

**Sales Touch:** Minimal - founder availability for questions, but mostly self-serve

---

### Post-MVP (Sales-Assisted)

**For larger deals ($5K+/mo):**
1. **Discovery Call:** Understand needs, pain points
2. **Demo:** Customized walkthrough
3. **Pilot:** 30-day paid pilot with 5 client profiles
4. **Close:** Annual contract, implementation kickoff
5. **CSM:** Dedicated customer success manager

## Launch Plan

### Pre-Launch (Weeks -4 to 0)

**Weeks -4 to -2: Beta Program**
- Recruit 5 beta users (from user interviews)
- Run closed beta
- Gather feedback, fix bugs
- Collect testimonials

**Weeks -2 to 0: Launch Prep**
- Finalize pricing and packaging

- Prepare launch content (blog post, LinkedIn posts)
- Set up analytics (Mixpanel, PostHog)

## Risks & Mitigation

### Risk 1: Low Willingness to Pay
**Risk:** Firms say "we'll just keep doing it manually"

**Mitigation:**
- Free trial to prove value
- ROI calculator showing time savings
- Case studies with $ impact

---

### Risk 2: Slow Sales Cycles
**Risk:** Accounting firms have long decision timelines (budget cycles)

**Mitigation:**
- Month-to-month pricing (no annual commitment required)
- Low-touch, self-serve trial
- Target firms mid-budget cycle

---

### Risk 3: Trust & Accuracy Concerns
**Risk:** "How do I know the AI won't miss something?"

**Mitigation:**
- Audit trail and transparency
- Testimonials from trusted peers
- Money-back guarantee if critical update missed
