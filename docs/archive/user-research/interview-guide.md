# User Interview Guide

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-04-18  
**Target:** Tax Partners, Compliance Managers, Tax Associates  
**Goal:** Validate problem, understand workflows, and identify feature priorities

---

## Interview Structure

**Duration:** 45-60 minutes  
**Format:** Video call (record with permission) or in-person  
**Preparation:** Send persona profile ahead of time for context

**Sections:**
1. Introduction & Context (5 min)
2. Current Workflow & Pain Points (20 min)
3. Regulatory Monitoring Deep Dive (15 min)
4. Client Impact Assessment (10 min)
5. Solution Feedback (10 min)
6. Wrap-up (5 min)

## Interview Script

### Introduction (5 min)

**Goal:** Build rapport, set expectations, get consent

**Script:**

"Hi [Name], thanks so much for taking the time to speak with me today. I'm [Your Name] from LedgerLens, and we're building a product called ReguLens to help tax professionals stay on top of regulatory changes.

Before we start, I want to mention:
- This conversation will take about 45-60 minutes
- There are no right or wrong answers - I'm here to learn from your experience
- Everything you share is confidential
- Would it be okay if I record this for internal notes? (if yes, start recording)

Great! To start, can you tell me a bit about your role and what your day-to-day looks like?"

**Notes to capture:**
- Role and responsibilities
- Firm size and structure
- Client portfolio size and types
- Years of experience

---

## Section 1: Current Workflow & Pain Points (20 min)

### Opening Question
"Walk me through how you currently stay informed about tax law changes that might affect your clients."

**Listen for:**
- Sources they check (websites, newsletters, databases)
- Frequency of checking (daily, weekly, monthly)
- Time spent on monitoring
- Tools they use
- Process for triaging updates

---

### Follow-up Questions

**On Sources:**
- "What sources do you check regularly?"
- "Are there sources you wish you had time to check but don't?"
- "Which sources do you trust most? Why?"
- "Do you subscribe to any email alerts or newsletters? Which ones?"

**On Frequency & Time:**
- "How often do you check each source?"
- "Roughly how much time do you spend per week on regulatory monitoring?"
- "Is this time billable or non-billable?"
- "When do you do this - mornings, evenings, weekends?"

**On Pain Points:**
- "What's most frustrating about the current process?"
- "Tell me about a time you missed an important update. What happened?"
- "What makes it hard to keep up with all the changes?"
- "If you could wave a magic wand, what would you change about this process?"

**Probe deeper with:**
- "Tell me more about that..."
- "Can you give me a specific example?"
- "What did you do then?"
- "How did that make you feel?"

---

## Section 2: Regulatory Monitoring Deep Dive (15 min)

### Discovery Process

**Questions:**
- "When you find a new tax bulletin or ruling, what do you do with it?"
- "How do you decide if it's relevant or can be ignored?"
- "Walk me through your process from 'I see a new CA FTB notice' to 'I know which clients are affected.'"

**Listen for:**
- Decision-making criteria
- Tools or spreadsheets used
- Collaboration with team
- Documentation practices

---

### Triage & Filtering

**Questions:**
- "What percentage of updates you see are actually relevant to your clients?"
- "What makes something 'high priority' vs. 'low priority'?"
- "How do you handle updates that *might* be relevant but you're not sure?"
- "Do you have a system for categorizing or tagging updates?"

**Listen for:**
- Signal-to-noise ratio
- False positive tolerance
- Uncertainty handling
- Organizational systems

---

### Multi-Jurisdiction Complexity

**Questions:**
- "How many states do you need to monitor for your clients?"
- "Is monitoring 5 states 5x harder than monitoring 1 state, or is it exponentially worse?"
- "Which states are hardest to monitor? Why?"
- "Have you ever had a client surprised by a requirement in a state you didn't realize they had nexus in?"

**Listen for:**
- Jurisdictional complexity
- Edge cases and surprises
- Mental models for multi-state

---

## Section 3: Client Impact Assessment (10 min)

### Matching Laws to Clients

**Questions:**
- "When you read about a new R&D credit expansion in California, how do you figure out which clients it affects?"
- "Do you have a system or database for tracking client attributes?"
- "What client information do you wish you had at your fingertips?"
- "Tell me about a time a client asked 'does this new law affect me?' How did you answer?"

**Listen for:**
- Mental models for matching
- Client data they track
- Gaps in current systems
- Use of tax software (UltraTax, CCH, etc.)

---

### Client Communication

**Questions:**
- "Once you identify a relevant change, how do you communicate it to the client?"
- "Do you proactively reach out, or wait for them to ask?"
- "What do clients expect from you regarding regulatory updates?"
- "Have you ever lost a client because a competitor surfaced an opportunity first?"

**Listen for:**
- Proactive vs. reactive posture
- Client expectations
- Competitive pressure
- Communication methods

---

## Section 4: Solution Feedback (10 min)

### Concept Introduction

**Script:**

"I want to share a concept with you and get your reaction. Imagine a system that:
- Automatically monitors all state tax websites and publications 24/7
- Uses AI to understand the *meaning* of new regulations, not just keywords
- Maps each change to your specific client profiles
- Sends you alerts like: 'This CA R&D credit expansion affects 3 of your clients: Client A, B, C'
- Provides a summary and action items, not just the raw legal text

What's your reaction to that?"

**Listen for:**
- Initial excitement or skepticism
- Questions they ask
- Concerns raised
- Features that resonate

---

### Feature Prioritization

**Questions:**
- "What would be most valuable: 100% coverage, or perfect accuracy on a smaller set of sources?"
- "Would you rather get 10 alerts where 8 are relevant, or risk missing 2 relevant ones?"
- "How important is it to see the 'why' behind an alert - like, how did the system decide this affects your client?"
- "Would you want a mobile app for on-the-go alerts, or is desktop/email fine?"

**Present trade-offs:**
- Coverage vs. Accuracy
- Speed vs. Depth
- Automation vs. Control

---

### Trust & Adoption

**Questions:**
- "What would make you trust a system like this?"
- "How would you verify the system didn't miss something important?"
- "Would your firm require specific security or compliance certifications?"
- "Who would need to approve a tool like this at your firm?"

**Listen for:**
- Trust requirements
- Verification needs
- Security concerns
- Buying process

---

### Pricing Sensitivity

**Questions:**
- "How much time would this save you per week?"
- "What's that time worth (billable rate or opportunity cost)?"
- "What do you currently pay for Bloomberg Tax or Checkpoint?"
- "If this saved you 10 hours/week, what would be a no-brainer price? What would be too expensive?"

**Listen for:**
- Willingness to pay
- Budget authority
- ROI calculation
- Pricing benchmarks

---

## Section 5: Wrap-up (5 min)

### Final Questions

**Questions:**
- "Is there anything I didn't ask that I should have?"
- "If you could build this yourself, what's the first feature you'd build?"
- "Who else should I talk to? (other tax professionals, compliance managers, etc.)"
- "Would you be interested in being part of a pilot program to test an early version?"

---

### Thank You & Next Steps

**Script:**

"This has been incredibly helpful. Thank you so much for your time and insights. 

A few next steps:
- I'll send you a summary of what I heard (for validation)
- As we develop the product, I'd love to get your feedback on early designs
- [If interested in pilot] I'll follow up in a few months about the pilot program

If you think of anything else after we hang up, feel free to email me at [email].

Thanks again!"
